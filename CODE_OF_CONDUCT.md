# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Saga should be a welcoming project for contributors and users of every background and level of technical experience. Be respectful, give specific and constructive feedback, and assume that a mistake can be corrected.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make unwanted sexual advances, disclose another person's private information, or attack people rather than discussing their work. Do not post private conversations, contact details or backups, even as examples. Respect requests to stop an interaction.

These standards apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces maintained for Saga, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and response

**Pre-launch gap:** a private conduct-reporting contact and the people responsible for handling reports have not yet been designated. The owner must establish that route and replace this paragraph before opening community spaces. Do not send sensitive reports to a public issue or use an invented email address.

Once that route is in place, reports should describe the behaviour, where it occurred and the minimum evidence necessary. Do not include unrelated personal data. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, consider conflicts of interest, and explain the next step to the reporter when safe to do so. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed absolutely.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may request a correction, remove content, limit participation, or exclude an account from project spaces. Apply these measures proportionately and consistently. This document does not promise a response deadline or an appeal process that the project has not staffed.
