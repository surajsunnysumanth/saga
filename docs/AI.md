# AI in Saga

All AI runs on your Mac. Saga never sends messages to a cloud model.

**Models** (picked automatically; choose in the sheet):
- Apple Intelligence (macOS 26+, on-device)
- Ollama (running on this Mac, port 11434): any model you've pulled, e.g. `ollama pull qwen2.5:3b`; for meaning search, an already-installed embedding model whose name contains “embed” (e.g. `nomic-embed-text`)
- LM Studio (local server, port 1234)
- Apple sentence embeddings (`NLEmbedding`, macOS 14+) or an Ollama embedding model, for meaning search only

**Features**
- *Catch me up* (chat header): opens a side panel. Summary of 7 days to a year, each point linking to the messages behind it. The panel stays open while you jump around, results are saved (reopen instantly, survive restarts and library rebuilds), and a stopped run resumes where it left off. Quick mode samples very long periods; Full detail reads everything.
- *Ask your archive* (the Ask pill, ⌘K): Saga finds messages by keyword, the model answers from them and cites sources. Keyword retrieval is always available and is the fallback; use the words people typed.
- *Meaning search* (the “Also match meaning” box inside Ask, **off by default**): a second retriever built from a one-time local index of sentence embeddings. It finds messages that mean the same thing without sharing words, then Ask fuses keyword and meaning results before answering. Choose Apple’s sentence embeddings (macOS language asset, nothing downloaded by Saga) or an embedding model already installed in Ollama on this Mac. The panel shows how much of the archive is indexed (empty / missing / partial / current), builds only when you press **Build index**, reports progress, and has **Stop**; finished work is kept and resumes. The index lives in `semantic_cache.db` and stores uids and vectors, never message text or contact names.
- *Topics in this chat* (chat header → **More** → “What this chat talked about”): a short, cited list of what a conversation covered in the last 7, 30, 90 or 365 days, anchored at the chat’s last archived message. Each topic links to one to three messages. Opening the sheet generates nothing: it shows a saved list if one exists (marked stale if the chat changed), and **Generate/Refresh** runs the local model over bounded parts with progress and **Stop**. Results are cached by archive, chat, period, model and an input fingerprint, and citations are resolved by uid against the current library, so a rebuild does not break the jumps.
- *Might need your reply* (Chats › ⋯ menu, or Settings › People & chats): deterministic, no model. It lists one-to-one chats whose latest substantive message is an incoming question that has been quiet for at least N whole days (adjustable, default 3) and was asked inside a recency window (adjustable, default the last 6 months), newest question first. Media-only replies count as an answer; groups, channels and chats without a known person are left out. A question older than the window is treated as history rather than a reply you still owe, and the sheet says how many it left out. Opening an item resolves its uid against the current library before jumping. It reads archived evidence only — it cannot know what you answered outside the archive, sends nothing and schedules nothing.


Notes: structured output (points + citations) is enforced; invented citations are dropped. Saved results, topic lists and vectors are stored by message uid, so they survive an index rebuild; row ids are never persisted. Small models (3B) are fast but sloppy: check sources with the arrows. Apple's safety filter may decline some chats; try an Ollama model then. Everything above runs on this Mac; the only network request in the AI text and embedding features is to Ollama on `127.0.0.1:11434`, and redirects are refused. (Music & video is separate: titles and artwork are fetched only when that toggle is on, as described below.)

CLI: `saga models`, `saga summarize --index vault_index.db --chat 180 --model <id>`, `saga ask --index vault_index.db "question" [--chat id] [--model id]`.

## Music & video from chats
*Music & video* (chat header, or the Music tile on Home for every chat) lists Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, YouTube Music and SoundCloud links shared in a chat, oldest first, grouped by month, with the official player one tap away (plays inside Saga). Only one plays at a time: starting a player pauses the others — YouTube and Spotify via their official pause APIs, the rest are unloaded with a reload button. Spotify `spotify.link` and SoundCloud `on.soundcloud.com` short links are expanded with one request only while titles are on; the target is cached in `link_meta.db`. Repeats are listed once ("shared 3×"), and a share link that expands to an item already listed counts as that item. Jump to the message, open in the service, copy the list, export it as M3U-style text or CSV, or open the YouTube items as a temporary playlist (first 50). Titles and artwork are off by default because they are fetched from Spotify/YouTube/SoundCloud; the toggle explains this. Spotify previews are 30 s unless you sign in inside the player; Apple Music private playlists may not embed.

## Wrapped by year
Wrapped (Insights, ⌘3) is the whole archive's year told as a story: the opening number with the days you turned up, a month-by-month chart that labels only the months in the data and says when the year is partial, who you talked to (conversations and groups, busiest first), the exchange that got the biggest laugh from a real quoted reply, the soundtrack (songs and videos shared that year, with titles, artwork and players), the longest message and what else you kept (photos, videos, voice notes), then a closing line. Jump arrows go back to the message; conversations open in the chat. Built on this Mac, no AI model needed, and cached by the year's newest message so switching years is instant. Titles and artwork are off by default because they are fetched from Spotify/YouTube/SoundCloud; the Titles switch in the bar explains this. Export (PDF or image) is the secondary action.

## Wrapped for two (per person)
Chat header (1:1 chats) or a person's page: *Wrapped*. Builds a story page for two people from their DM: first message, who carries the chat (messages, conversation starters, reply speed), the laugh track (messages that got a laugh back, who is funnier), the soundtrack (songs/videos swapped, which ones landed, on repeat, shared by both, with players), inside jokes (phrases you use far more than the rest of the archive), signature words and emojis, months over the years, longest silence, streaks. Everything is computed on this Mac, no AI model needed (about 2 s). Save as a single web page or PDF to send to the other person (it contains short quotes). CLI: `saga duo --index vault_index.db --chat <id> --me Name --out wrapped.html`.

## Merging contacts
Settings › People & chats › Merge contacts, or "Merge with another number" on a person page. Suggestions come with the evidence behind them (same saved name, same WhatsApp profile name, numbers that end alike, or one chat going quiet as another starts) and a plain confidence tier; ignore one and restore it later. Manual merge of any two people (search unnamed old numbers by digits) still works. Stored in the vault (config/merges.json, config/merge-suggestion-ignores.json), applied on rebuild, undo with Unmerge. Details: `docs/CONTACT-SUGGESTIONS.md`.
